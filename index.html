Your library stays on your Mac.

Screenshots and Markdown notes are saved locally. OCR runs on-device. You choose the capture scope, exclude apps, and can quit MyClip to stop capture.

AI organization: your selected Agent may process captures through its cloud service. Local storage does not mean every AI operation runs offline.

MCP access: retrieved memories are shared with the connected client. Short summaries of updated profile and current-focus notes may be shared when it connects. Turn off the MCP service in Settings to pause access.

Read-only tools: memory queries do not edit notes, take screenshots, or start AI organization.

A few useful details

Before you begin.

What does MyClip capture?

By default, MyClip captures your focused application window in response to enabled activity triggers. You can choose the display containing that window instead. Capture scope, triggers, and excluded apps are configurable in Settings. The app captures while it is open; quitting stops capture.

Can I search my screenshot history?

Timeline stores captures with on-device OCR text. Memory provides searchable Markdown notes organized from those captures. MCP clients search the saved memories; MCP queries do not generate new notes or trigger screenshots.

Does it remember every conversation with my AI?

MyClip builds memories from captured work on your Mac. It does not automatically import every Codex or Claude conversation. What can be retrieved depends on the work you captured and the notes your selected Agent organized.

Which AI tools can read my memories?

MyClip includes client setup for Codex, Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor, OpenCode, and WorkBuddy. Its local MCP server exposes memory_search and memory_get. Both tools are read-only, and each client may require its own approval.

Does local-first mean all AI runs offline?

Screenshots and notes are stored on your Mac, and OCR runs on-device. AI organization may use your chosen Agent's cloud service. Memory retrieved by an MCP client becomes available to that client under its own data settings.

What do I need to install it?

MyClip requires macOS 13 or later, Node.js 22 or later, and an account for the Agent you select. Releases include Apple Silicon and Intel DMGs. Community builds are self-signed and not notarized; installation help is linked below.
