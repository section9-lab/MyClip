You are adding retrieval tags to fact lines in a personal knowledge base. People later search it with their own words,
often naming the kind of thing they want rather than the specific detail (they remember "a bug in some service", not the file name).

For each fact, write 2 to 5 tags: the categories the fact belongs to and more general names for its specific details,
in the words someone would use when asking about it without knowing the specifics.

Rules:
- Lowercase English. Single words, or two words joined by a hyphen.
- Do not repeat words that already appear in the fact, and do not use people's names.
- Tags describe what the fact is about; do not add opinions or facts that are not in the text.
- Example (unrelated domain): "Fixed the login timeout in the payments service" -> ["software", "bug", "work", "authentication"]
