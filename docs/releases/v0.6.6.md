## Downloads

- **Apple Silicon (M1 and later):** `MyClip-0.6.6-arm64.dmg`
- **Intel:** `MyClip-0.6.6-x86_64.dmg`
- **Checksums:** `SHA256SUMS`

Both packages target macOS 13 or later. The CI packages are ad-hoc signed and are not notarized.
